HOW TO APPLY

A better way to apply.

Three small steps before you send that application.

  1. 1

    Find a relevant role

    Open the careers page. Filter for Chennai, your skills and your experience level.

  2. 2

    Find the right person

    Use the LinkedIn recruiter search to find people in talent acquisition. Verify their employer before reaching out.

  3. 3

    Send a specific message

    Share the role link, why your experience fits and one strong example of your work. Keep it short.

Good to know.

Are these companies hiring right now?

Not necessarily. We share companies worth researching. Live vacancies, eligibility and deadlines change, so confirm details on each company's official careers website before applying.

Are these direct HR contacts?

“Find recruiters” opens a LinkedIn search for relevant public profiles. These are search results, not verified HR contacts or guaranteed referrals. Never pay anyone to secure an interview.

Can freshers use this list?

Yes, as a starting point. Check each company's student, graduate or entry-level hiring channels. Not all companies have fresher roles open at all times.
